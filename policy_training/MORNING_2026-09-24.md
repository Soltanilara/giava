## What the head does (policy_training/act_sorterhead.py)

A 1×1 conv on the top_scene backbone map → three linear read-outs, one per
piece → `(dx, dy, found)` for the piece **this episode's task string names**.
Label = `observation.environment_state[6, 7, 2]`, which rides in the batch but
is never a policy input. Only the target piece's read-out gets a gradient, so
on a triangle episode the triangle output must fire on the triangle while the
cube and flower are also in frame — that's the identity binding. At rollout
the head is never called; `select_action` is inherited from ACT unchanged.

  at ~0.8×std. Well below that = it's localizing. Per-piece numbers should
  all drop; if they're equal and large it found "a blob", not the piece.

  test; if the control's ever moves, something is wrong.
- **found acc** — w0 stays at chance (~0.5–0.6); w1 should climb well past it.

checkpoint (11/32), because it reads the task string. Putting the same head
on it is the version of this experiment that matters most — but the head
